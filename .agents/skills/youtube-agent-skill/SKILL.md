# YouTube Agent Skill (`youtube-agent-skill`)

A specialized production skill designed to plan, script, storyboard, optimize, and publish high-impact YouTube video content for **Dental Pro Clinic & SaaS ERP**.

---

## 🎯 Core Capabilities

1. **Structured Scriptwriting (YouTube Scripts)**:
   - Attention-grabbing Hooks (first 5-10 seconds to maximize audience retention).
   - Problem → Agitation → Solution structure tailored for dentists, clinic managers, and receptionists.
   - Clear Calls to Action (CTA) for demo bookings, subscriptions, or website visits.

2. **Visual Storyboarding & Screen Direction**:
   - Exact screen recording cues for tools like **Screen Studio**, **OBS**, or **Loom**.
   - Clear UI navigation steps: where to click, zoom targets, highlight callouts.

3. **AI Voiceover Ready Formatting**:
   - Optimized phonetic pacing for **ElevenLabs** and **HeyGen** text-to-speech.
   - SSML-friendly pauses (`...` / `[وقفة قصيرة]`), tone markers, and natural Arabic articulation.

4. **YouTube SEO & Publishing Kit**:
   - 3-5 high-CTR title variations (emotional, curiosity, and search-driven).
   - Rich video descriptions with clickable Timestamps / Chapters.
   - High-volume search tags and dental software keywords.
   - Creative thumbnail prompts and copy concepts.

5. **YouTube Shorts & Instagram Reels Variations**:
   - 30-60 second vertical video adaptations emphasizing single "magic moment" features (e.g. 1-click WhatsApp appointment reminders, interactive odontogram, instant invoicing).

## 🎬 Video Formats & Templates

### 1. Full Product Walkthrough (5 - 8 Minutes)
- **Target Audience**: Clinic owners and medical directors looking for an all-in-one clinic management system.
- **Structure**:
  - `00:00 - 00:30`: The Hook (the chaos of manual dental booking and paperwork vs automated smart clinic).
  - `00:30 - 02:00`: Patient EMR & Interactive Odontogram.
  - `02:00 - 03:30`: Automated Scheduling (1-click WhatsApp + Google Calendar Sync).
  - `03:30 - 05:00`: Dental Billing, Payments & Patient Ledger.
  - `05:00 - 06:30`: Analytics, Doctors Performance & Multi-Clinic Management.
  - `06:30 - 07:00`: Outro & CTA (Free Demo / Website Link).

### 2. Feature Spotlight / Quick Tutorial (1 - 3 Minutes)
- **Target Audience**: Dentists, receptionists, or administrative staff.
- **Focus**: A single killer feature (e.g. *كيف ترسل تذكيرات المواعيد تلقائياً عبر واتساب وتقويم Google بدون أي مجهود*).

### 3. YouTube Shorts / TikTok / Reels (30 - 60 Seconds)
- **Format**: 9:16 Vertical.
- **Pacing**: Fast-paced, punchy audio, continuous on-screen movement.
- **Structure**:
  - 3s Hook: "توقف عن خسارة المرضى بسبب نسيان المواعيد!"
  - 20s Screen Action: Click book appointment -> instant WhatsApp & Google Calendar invite delivered.
  - 7s CTA: رابط التجربة في أول تعليق.

## 🎙️ AI Voiceover Guidelines (Arabic)

When generating voiceover scripts for **ElevenLabs**, **HeyGen**, or **Synthesia**:
- Write in clear, professional Modern Standard Arabic (فصحى مبسطة ومعاصرة) or natural white dialect (لهجة بيضاء مقبولة).
- Add phonetic diacritics (تشكيل) on ambiguous words to ensure perfect AI pronunciation:
  - مثال: `نِظام Dental Pro` ، `مُخَطَّط الأسنان` ، `التذكيرات التلقائية`.
- Insert natural breathing pauses using punctuation (ellipsis `...` or commas) to prevent the AI from rushing through technical terms.
